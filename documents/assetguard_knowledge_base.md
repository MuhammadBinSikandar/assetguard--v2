SECTION 1: PLATFORM OVERVIEW

What is AssetGuard?
AssetGuard is a blockchain-powered real estate platform that lets users buy, sell, and invest in properties using AI, smart contracts, and the Solana blockchain. It makes real estate faster, safer, and accessible from as little as $100.

What problems does AssetGuard solve?
AssetGuard reduces property transfer time from 7–9 weeks to under 2 hours, cuts transaction costs by up to 70%, eliminates fraud risk by 95%, and enables fractional investing starting at $100.

Who can use AssetGuard?
AssetGuard is built for three types of users: Investors (buy properties or fractions), Sellers (list and tokenize properties), and Admins (verify users and approve listings).

Is AssetGuard available on mobile?
Yes — AssetGuard is a web-based platform that works on any modern browser across desktop and mobile, with no app installation needed.

What blockchain does AssetGuard use?
AssetGuard runs on the Solana blockchain, chosen for its ultra-low fees (~$0.00025 per transaction) and fast confirmation times (under 5 seconds).

SECTION 2: ACCOUNT & REGISTRATION

How do I create an account on AssetGuard?
Click Sign Up, enter your name, email, and password, verify your email, then complete KYC to unlock full access.

What is KYC and why is it required?
KYC (Know Your Customer) is a mandatory identity verification process required by law to prevent fraud and money laundering.

What happens after I sign up without completing KYC?
You can browse listings but cannot buy, sell, or register properties until your KYC is approved.

Can I use AssetGuard without a crypto wallet?
You can browse the platform without a wallet, but a connected Phantom wallet is required to buy or sell property tokens.

SECTION 3: KYC VERIFICATION

What is the KYC process on AssetGuard?
You upload a valid government-issued ID, complete a facial scan, and submit for admin review — you'll be notified once a decision is made.

What documents are accepted for KYC?
AssetGuard accepts a Passport, CNIC, or Driver's License — make sure your document is valid and clearly readable.

How long does KYC approval take?
KYC is typically approved within 1–2 hours, though it can be instant depending on admin workload.

How will I know if my KYC is approved?
You'll receive a platform notification and email, and a verification badge will appear on your profile once approved.

How do I check my KYC status?
Log in, go to your Profile or Settings page, and check the KYC Status section — it will show Pending, Approved, or Rejected.

What does each KYC status mean?
Pending means your documents are under review; Approved means full access is unlocked; Rejected means you need to correct and resubmit your documents.

What if my KYC is rejected?
You'll receive a reason for rejection and can resubmit corrected documents through the KYC section in your profile.

Common reasons for KYC rejection:
Documents are commonly rejected for being blurry, expired, mismatched with the face scan, or being the wrong document type.

Is my KYC data safe?
Yes — all identity documents are encrypted and stored securely, and are never shared with third parties without your consent.

SECTION 4: PROPERTY REGISTRATION

Who can register a property on AssetGuard?
Any KYC-verified user can register a property as a seller.

How do I register my property on AssetGuard?
Go to Register Property, fill in your property details, upload the required documents, and submit — once approved, your property is tokenized on Solana and an AG Token is issued.

What documents are required to register a property?
You need to upload a Title Deed and a valid ID proof (Passport, CNIC, or Driver's License).

How long does property approval take?
Property approval typically takes 1–2 hours, and you'll receive a notification once a decision is made.

What is an AG Token?
An AG Token is a unique digital token on the Solana blockchain representing ownership (or fractional ownership) of a verified property on AssetGuard.

What happens after my property is approved?
Your property is listed on the marketplace, an AG Token is sent to your Phantom wallet, and you can sell it fully or offer fractions.

What if my property registration is rejected?
You'll be notified with a reason and can correct the issue and resubmit at any time.

Can I register multiple properties?
Yes — there is no limit on how many properties a verified seller can register.

Can I edit a property listing after it's approved?
Some details can be edited from My Listings; others tied to verified documents may require resubmission.

How does blockchain protect my property record?
Your property data and a document hash are stored on the Solana blockchain, making them immutable and tamper-proof.

SECTION 6: BUYING PROPERTY FRACTIONS

What is fractional ownership?
Fractional ownership lets you buy a small share of a property represented by AG Tokens, instead of purchasing the entire property.

What is the minimum investment to buy property fractions?
The minimum investment is $100.

Can I buy multiple tokens (fractions) of the same property?
Yes — you can buy as many available fractions as you want from the same property.

How do I buy property fractions on AssetGuard?
Ensure your KYC is approved and Phantom wallet is connected, then browse listings, select a property, choose the number of fractions, and confirm the transaction in your wallet.

Where do my purchased tokens appear?
Your AG Tokens appear in both your Phantom wallet and your AssetGuard dashboard under My Portfolio.

What payment methods are accepted?
Only cryptocurrency payments via the Solana blockchain are accepted — no fiat payments (credit card, bank transfer, etc.).

Which cryptocurrency do I use to buy fractions?
You use SOL (Solana's native currency) to pay for transactions, and AG Tokens represent your property ownership.

How fast is the purchase transaction?
Transactions confirm on the Solana blockchain in under 5 seconds — nearly instant.

Can I sell my fractions later?
Yes — you can list your fractions for sale anytime through the Partial Liquidation feature on the marketplace.

What happens to my fractions if a property is sold completely?
All fractional token holders are notified and the smart contract manages settlement automatically.

What is Partial Liquidation?
Partial Liquidation lets property owners sell a portion of their property as tokens to raise cash while retaining partial ownership.

Why would a seller use Partial Liquidation?
It lets sellers raise liquidity without selling the full property or taking out a bank loan.

How do I list my property fractions for sale?
Go to My Listings, select a property, choose how many fractions to sell and set a price, then submit — buyers can purchase instantly and the smart contract handles the transfer.

How long does it take to sell fractions?
There's no fixed timeline — it depends on investor demand, but transfers happen instantly once a buyer completes a purchase.

Do I get notified when someone buys my fractions?
Yes — you receive a real-time notification confirming the sale and token transfer.

SECTION 8: OWNERSHIP TRANSFER

How does a full property ownership transfer work on AssetGuard?
The buyer sends payment to a smart contract escrow, which automatically verifies conditions, transfers the AG Token to the buyer's wallet, and releases funds to the seller — all in under 2 hours.

Who initiates the ownership transfer?
The transfer is automatically triggered by the smart contract when the buyer completes payment — no manual action is needed from the seller.

How long does a full ownership transfer take?
The entire process takes under 2 hours, with blockchain confirmation happening in under 5 seconds.

What if the transfer fails midway?
The smart contract automatically refunds the buyer's full payment — no funds are lost.

Can I check the status of a transfer in real-time?
Yes — both buyers and sellers can track transfer status (Pending, In Progress, Completed, or Failed) on the platform.

Is there a record of every ownership change?
Yes — every transfer is permanently recorded on the Solana blockchain with a timestamp, viewable via the Blockchain Explorer.

SECTION 11: SMART CONTRACTS & BLOCKCHAIN

What is a smart contract?
A smart contract is a self-executing digital agreement that automatically carries out actions (payments, transfers, escrow) when set conditions are met — no middleman needed.

Why does AssetGuard use smart contracts?
Smart contracts make transactions faster, cheaper (up to 70% cost savings), safer, and fully transparent.

What is escrow and how does it work on AssetGuard?
The smart contract locks the buyer's funds in escrow and only releases them to the seller once the property token is successfully transferred; if anything fails, the buyer is automatically refunded.

What is the Blockchain Explorer?
It's a public tool on AssetGuard that lets anyone search and view transactions, property token details, ownership history, and wallet activity.

Are blockchain records permanent?
Yes — once written to the Solana blockchain, records are immutable and cannot be changed or deleted by anyone.

SECTION 14: TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ISSUES

My KYC is taking longer than 2 hours. What should I do?
Check your notification inbox for any update or resubmission request — during busy periods, review may take slightly longer than usual.

My wallet won't connect. What should I try?
Make sure Phantom is installed and unlocked, refresh the page, disable any ad-blockers, and try disconnecting and reconnecting from Phantom's settings.

I sent payment but haven't received my tokens. What happened?
Check your transaction status in My Portfolio — if it failed, the smart contract will auto-refund you; if it's stuck on "Pending," it may be due to high network traffic.

I can't find a specific property. How do I search?
Use the search and filter tools on the Browse Properties page to filter by location, price, type, or search by property name or token ID.

Why can't I buy fractions even though I'm logged in?
Ensure your KYC is approved, your Phantom wallet is connected, and your wallet has sufficient SOL for the purchase and fees.

I accidentally disconnected my wallet. Did I lose my tokens?
No — your tokens are stored on the Solana blockchain and are tied to your wallet address, not AssetGuard's servers. Simply reconnect your Phantom wallet.
