## SECTION 1: PLATFORM OVERVIEW

### What is AssetGuard?
AssetGuard is a blockchain-powered real estate platform that allows users to register properties, invest in fractional property ownership, transfer ownership securely, and track all transactions transparently. It combines Artificial Intelligence (AI), blockchain technology (Solana), and smart contracts to make real estate investing faster, safer, and accessible to everyone — even with a small budget.

### What problems does AssetGuard solve?
- Traditional property transfers take 7–9 weeks. AssetGuard reduces this to under 2 hours.
- Real estate fraud is common. AssetGuard reduces fraud risk by 95% using blockchain verification.
- Investing in real estate usually requires a lot of money. AssetGuard allows investments starting from as little as $100.
- Middlemen like brokers charge 5–6% commission. AssetGuard cuts transaction costs by up to 70% using smart contracts.
- Property valuations are slow and inaccurate. AssetGuard provides AI-powered valuations with ±2% precision in under 5 seconds.
- Ownership records are often unclear. AssetGuard stores all records on blockchain for full transparency.

### Who can use AssetGuard?
- **Investors**: People who want to buy whole or fractional properties and earn returns.
- **Sellers**: Property owners who want to list, tokenize, and sell their property or fractions of it.
- **Admins**: Platform staff who verify users, approve properties, and monitor system activity.

### Is AssetGuard available on mobile?
AssetGuard is a web-based platform accessible from any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) on desktop and mobile devices including Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS. No app installation is required.

### What blockchain does AssetGuard use?
AssetGuard uses the **Solana blockchain**. Solana is chosen for its extremely low transaction fees (approximately $0.00025 per transaction) and fast confirmation times (under 5 seconds), making it ideal for real estate transactions.

## SECTION 2: ACCOUNT & REGISTRATION

### How do I create an account on AssetGuard?
1. Visit the AssetGuard platform and click **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your name, email address, and a strong password.
3. Verify your email address through the confirmation link sent to your inbox.
4. Complete the KYC (Know Your Customer) verification process to unlock full platform access.

### What is KYC and why is it required?
KYC stands for **Know Your Customer**. It is a mandatory identity verification process required by law to prevent fraud, money laundering, and illegal activity. Without completing KYC, users cannot buy, sell, or register properties on AssetGuard.

### What happens after I sign up without completing KYC?
You will have limited access to the platform. You can browse property listings and explore the platform, but you cannot make transactions, register properties, or buy fractions until your KYC is approved.

### Can I use AssetGuard without a crypto wallet?
You need a connected crypto wallet (Phantom) to buy or sell property tokens. However, you can browse listings and view platform features without a wallet connected.

## SECTION 3: KYC VERIFICATION

### What is the KYC process on AssetGuard?
KYC is a step-by-step identity verification process that involves:
1. Uploading a valid government-issued ID document.
2. Completing a facial verification scan (your face is cross-checked with your ID).
3. Submitting the application for admin review.
4. Receiving approval or rejection with feedback.

### What documents are accepted for KYC?
AssetGuard accepts the following government-issued identity documents:
- **Passport**
- **CNIC (Computerized National Identity Card)**
- **Driver's License**

Make sure your document is valid (not expired), clearly readable, and the photo matches your face.

### How long does KYC approval take?
KYC applications are reviewed by an AssetGuard admin. Approval typically takes **1–2 hours**, but in some cases it can be **instant** depending on workload. You will receive a notification once your KYC is reviewed.

### How will I know if my KYC is approved?
You will receive a notification on the platform and via email when your KYC status changes. Once approved, you will receive a **digital verification badge** on your profile, and full platform access will be unlocked.

### How do I check my KYC status?
1. Log in to your AssetGuard account.
2. Go to your **Profile** or **Settings** page.
3. Look for the **KYC Status** section — it will show one of the following: **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**.

### What does each KYC status mean?
- **Pending**: Your documents have been submitted and are waiting for admin review.
- **Approved**: Your identity has been verified. You now have full access to all platform features.
- **Rejected**: Your documents could not be verified. You will receive a reason and can resubmit.

### What if my KYC is rejected?
If your KYC is rejected, don't worry — you can **resubmit**. You will receive feedback explaining why it was rejected (e.g., blurry document, face mismatch). Simply correct the issue and upload your documents again through the KYC section in your profile.

### Common reasons for KYC rejection:
- Document image is blurry or unclear.
- Document is expired.
- Face in the live scan does not match the ID photo.
- Uploaded the wrong type of document.
- Document details are not fully visible.

### Is my KYC data safe?
Yes. All identity documents are stored securely and encrypted. AssetGuard follows strict data protection policies. Your documents are only used for identity verification purposes and are never shared with third parties without your consent.

## SECTION 4: PROPERTY REGISTRATION

### Who can register a property on AssetGuard?
Any user who has completed KYC verification can register a property on AssetGuard as a seller.

### How do I register my property on AssetGuard?
1. Log in and navigate to **Register Property**.
2. Fill in the property details (location, size, type, price, etc.).
3. Upload the required documents.
4. Submit for admin review.
5. Once approved, your property is tokenized on the Solana blockchain and a unique **AG token** is issued representing your property.

### What documents are required to register a property?
You need to upload the following documents:
- **Title Deed** (proof of legal ownership of the property)
- **Owner's ID Proof** (same as accepted KYC documents: Passport, CNIC, or Driver's License)

Make sure all documents are clear, valid, and match the property details you enter in the form.

### How long does property approval take?
Property documents are reviewed manually by an AssetGuard admin. The approval time is similar to KYC — typically **1–2 hours**, sometimes instant. You will receive a notification once a decision is made.

### What is an AG Token?
An **AG Token** is a unique digital token issued on the Solana blockchain when a property is successfully registered and verified on AssetGuard. Each AG Token represents ownership (or fractional ownership) of a specific real estate property. It acts as a digital proof of ownership that is tamper-proof and publicly verifiable.

### What happens after my property is approved?
- Your property is listed on the AssetGuard marketplace.
- An AG Token is created and stored in your connected Phantom wallet.
- You can choose to sell the full property or offer fractions for partial liquidation.
- A verification badge is displayed on your property listing.

### What if my property registration is rejected?
You will be notified with a reason. You can **correct the issue** (e.g., upload clearer documents or fix incorrect details) and resubmit your property for approval.

### Can I register multiple properties?
Yes. There is no limit on the number of properties a verified seller can register on AssetGuard.

### Can I edit a property listing after it's approved?
You can manage your listings from the **My Listings** page. Some details may be editable; others may require resubmission if they are tied to verified documents.

### How does blockchain protect my property record?
Once your property is approved and tokenized, all ownership data and a hash (digital fingerprint) of your documents are stored on the Solana blockchain. This data is **immutable** — meaning it cannot be changed, deleted, or forged — providing a permanent, transparent ownership record.

## SECTION 6: BUYING PROPERTY FRACTIONS

### What is fractional ownership?
Fractional ownership means you don't have to buy an entire property. Instead, you can buy a **fraction (a small share)** of a property represented by AG Tokens. This allows people to invest in real estate with a much smaller budget.

### What is the minimum investment to buy property fractions?
The minimum investment on AssetGuard is **$100**. This makes real estate investing accessible to everyone, not just wealthy investors.

### Can I buy multiple tokens (fractions) of the same property?
Yes! You can buy as many fractions as you want, as long as they are available. For example, if a property has 100 tokens available, you can buy 10 tokens and own 10% of that property.

### How do I buy property fractions on AssetGuard?

**Step-by-step guide:**
1. **Complete KYC**: Make sure your identity is verified and your KYC status is "Approved."
2. **Connect your Phantom Wallet**: Go to wallet settings and connect your Phantom wallet.
3. **Browse Listings**: Go to **Browse Properties** and use filters to find properties you're interested in.
4. **View Property Details**: Click on a listing to see details, price per fraction, total fractions available, and AI valuation.
5. **Click "Buy Fractions"**: Select the number of fractions you want to purchase.
6. **Confirm Payment**: Review the total cost and confirm the transaction using your Phantom wallet.
7. **Smart Contract Executes**: The smart contract automatically processes the payment and transfers the tokens to your wallet.
8. **Receive Tokens**: The AG Tokens representing your purchased fractions appear in both your **Phantom wallet** and your **AssetGuard dashboard**.

### Where do my purchased tokens appear?
Your purchased AG Tokens are visible in **two places**:
- Your **Phantom wallet** (as on-chain tokens on the Solana blockchain)
- Your **AssetGuard dashboard** under **My Portfolio**

### What payment methods are accepted?
AssetGuard currently supports **cryptocurrency payments only** on the Solana blockchain. No fiat payments (credit card, bank transfer, PayPal) are accepted at this time.

### Which cryptocurrency do I use to buy fractions?
You use **SOL (Solana's native currency)** to pay for transactions and fees. The property tokens themselves are **AG Tokens** issued by AssetGuard on the Solana blockchain.

### How fast is the purchase transaction?
Transactions on the Solana blockchain are confirmed in **under 5 seconds**, making buying property fractions almost instant.

### Can I sell my fractions later?
Yes! You can list your fractions for sale on the AssetGuard marketplace at any time through the **Partial Liquidation** feature. Other investors can browse and buy your fractions.

### What happens to my fractions if a property is sold completely?
If a property undergoes a full ownership transfer, all fractional token holders are notified and the smart contract manages the settlement accordingly.

## SECTION 7: SELLING PROPERTY FRACTIONS (PARTIAL LIQUIDATION)

### What is Partial Liquidation?
Partial Liquidation is a feature that allows property owners to **sell a portion of their property** instead of selling it entirely or taking out a loan. By tokenizing the property, sellers can offer fractions to investors and receive liquidity while retaining partial ownership.

### Why would a seller use Partial Liquidation?
- To raise cash without selling the entire property.
- To avoid high-interest bank loans using property as collateral.
- To share risk with other investors while staying involved in the property.

### How do I list my property fractions for sale?
1. Go to **My Listings** or **My Portfolio**.
2. Select the property you want to partially liquidate.
3. Choose how many fractions (tokens) you want to sell and set a price per fraction.
4. Submit the listing — it will appear on the marketplace for investors to browse and buy.
5. When an investor buys your fractions, the smart contract automatically transfers the tokens and credits your wallet.

### How long does it take to sell fractions?
There is no fixed timeline — it depends on investor demand. Once listed, your fractions are visible to all investors on the platform. When a buyer completes a purchase, the transfer happens instantly via smart contract.

### Do I get notified when someone buys my fractions?
Yes. You receive a **real-time notification** when a buyer purchases your fractions, confirming the sale and token transfer.

## SECTION 8: OWNERSHIP TRANSFER

### How does a full property ownership transfer work on AssetGuard?
AssetGuard uses smart contracts to fully automate ownership transfers:
1. A buyer selects a property and agrees to the purchase terms.
2. The buyer sends payment (in SOL) to a **secure escrow** managed by the smart contract.
3. The smart contract verifies that payment and all conditions are met.
4. The AG Token (property ownership token) is **automatically transferred** from the seller's wallet to the buyer's wallet on the Solana blockchain.
5. Both buyer and seller receive a **digital confirmation** with full transaction details.
6. The ownership record on the blockchain is updated in real-time.

### Who initiates the ownership transfer?
The transfer is **automatically triggered by the smart contract** when the buyer completes payment. No manual action is needed from the seller — the entire process is automated.

### How long does a full ownership transfer take?
The entire process takes **under 2 hours** in most cases. Blockchain transaction confirmation happens in **under 5 seconds**, and the rest of the time accounts for verification steps.

### What if the transfer fails midway?
If a transfer fails for any reason, the smart contract **automatically refunds** the buyer's funds. The money is returned to the buyer's wallet without requiring any manual intervention. No funds are lost.

### Can I check the status of a transfer in real-time?
Yes. Both buyers and sellers can track transfer status in real-time on the platform. Status updates include: **Pending**, **In Progress**, **Completed**, or **Failed**.

### Is there a record of every ownership change?
Yes. Every ownership transfer is permanently recorded on the Solana blockchain with a timestamp. Anyone can verify this history using the **Blockchain Explorer** on the platform.

## SECTION 11: SMART CONTRACTS & BLOCKCHAIN

### What is a smart contract?
A smart contract is a **self-executing digital agreement** programmed to automatically carry out actions when certain conditions are met — without needing a middleman. On AssetGuard, smart contracts handle payments, token transfers, escrow, and partial liquidations automatically and securely.

### Why does AssetGuard use smart contracts?
Smart contracts make transactions:
- **Faster**: No need to wait for banks or lawyers.
- **Cheaper**: No broker or middleman fees (saves up to 70% in costs).
- **Safer**: Funds are held in escrow and only released when all conditions are met.
- **Transparent**: All actions are recorded permanently on the blockchain.

### What is escrow and how does it work on AssetGuard?
Escrow is a secure holding mechanism where a buyer's funds are locked by the smart contract until the conditions of the sale are fully met. Once the property token is transferred to the buyer's wallet, the funds are automatically released to the seller. If anything goes wrong, the smart contract refunds the buyer automatically.

### What is the Blockchain Explorer?
The **Blockchain Explorer** is a tool on AssetGuard that lets anyone publicly search and view:
- All transactions on the platform
- Property token details and ownership history
- Wallet addresses and their transaction logs
- Asset verification and token authenticity

You can search by **property token ID**, **wallet address**, or **transaction ID**.

### Are blockchain records permanent?
Yes. Once a transaction or ownership record is written to the Solana blockchain, it is **immutable** — it cannot be changed, deleted, or tampered with by anyone, including AssetGuard admins.

## SECTION 14: TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ISSUES

### My KYC is taking longer than 2 hours. What should I do?
KYC is reviewed manually by an admin and typically takes 1–2 hours. During busy periods it may take slightly longer. If it has been significantly longer, check your notification inbox for any update or resubmission request from the admin.

### My wallet won't connect. What should I try?
- Make sure the **Phantom wallet** extension is installed and unlocked in your browser.
- Try refreshing the page and clicking **Connect Wallet** again.
- Make sure your browser is up to date (Chrome or Firefox recommended).
- Disable any ad-blockers that may be interfering with the wallet popup.
- Try disconnecting and reconnecting the wallet from Phantom's settings.

### I sent payment but haven't received my tokens. What happened?
Solana transactions confirm in under 5 seconds. If you haven't received your tokens:
- Check your transaction status in **My Portfolio** or **Transaction History**.
- If the transaction failed, the smart contract will automatically refund your payment.
- If the status shows "Pending" for more than a few minutes, it may be due to high blockchain network traffic (a known limitation).

### I can't find a specific property. How do I search?
Use the **search and filter tools** on the Browse Properties page. You can filter by location, price range, property type, fraction availability, and more. You can also search directly using a property name or token ID.

### Why can't I buy fractions even though I'm logged in?
Make sure:
1. Your **KYC is approved** (status shows "Approved" in your profile).
2. Your **Phantom wallet is connected** to AssetGuard.
3. Your wallet has **sufficient SOL** for the purchase and transaction fees.

### I accidentally disconnected my wallet. Did I lose my tokens?
No. Your tokens are stored safely on the Solana blockchain — they are tied to your wallet address, not to AssetGuard's servers. Reconnect your Phantom wallet to AssetGuard to see your tokens again.
